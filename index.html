How it works

1 · tribute

Put ETH behind any Pons coin.

Holders, creators, anyone. A coin's odds are its share of the pot: 1 ETH in a 4 ETH pot is 25%. No cap. Pay in ETH or PONS.

2 · the Nod

Every 10 minutes, if the pot is 0.5 ETH or more.

Below that, the round rolls over and the pot keeps growing. The coin is picked by a drand beacon, verified on-chain in the same transaction that buys it. Nobody can predict it, nobody can wait for it.

3 · the take

The pot buys the coin. Its backers split the tokens.

Bought in one shot at the Nod, so no bot can front-run it. Tokens unlock in 4 parts over 4 hours. Only the wallets that paid tribute to that coin get them, pro-rata.

4 · your CUT

Every ETH of tribute mines CUT. Win or lose.

Points, pro-rata to what you put in. The Envelope counts too. CUT points convert 1:1 to $CUT at TGE. No presale, no team allocation. Hold CUT, get a taste of every round in ETH.

The Score: 2% of every pot goes into the Score before the split above. Each Nod has a 1 in 100 chance of firing it. When it fires, 80% is paid in ETH to that round's winning backers on top of their tokens. It never resets to zero.
The Envelope: ETH added to the pot behind no coin. Counts for nobody's odds, goes entirely to the winning backers. For Pons, for creators heating up their coin's hour, for anyone. Mines CUT like any tribute.
Creators: the pot's buy pays your Pons creator tax like any other buy. The bigger your tax, the more each Nod pays you.
Small coins: a pot can move a tiny coin a lot. Look at the market cap before you pay tribute. Nothing is refunded. Send from your own wallet.
$CUT trade tax: 2%, half to the treasury, half to locked CUT. It can only go down.
